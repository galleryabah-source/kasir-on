# KASIRA Pricing Hypothesis — Phase 0

## Packaging principle

Charge for business value and operational scale, not for basic data ownership.

## Proposed tiers

### Starter
One outlet, core POS, basic reports, basic offline capability.

### Pro
Inventory, multiple terminals, advanced permissions, reconciliation, richer reports.

### Premium
Multi-outlet, advanced analytics, CRM, AI insight, integrations.

### Enterprise
SLA, advanced security, franchise controls, custom integrations, dedicated support.

## Pricing experiments

Test:
- per outlet/month
- per terminal/month
- base subscription + outlet add-on
- annual discount
- paid onboarding for higher tiers

## Metrics

- activation to first sale
- time to first successful closing
- conversion trial -> paid
- ARPU
- gross margin after support/payment costs
- churn
- expansion revenue

## Decision rule

Do not freeze price solely from competitor price pages. Validate willingness-to-pay with target users and pilot economics.
